## 1. Chat Commands
**Slug:** `chat-commands` | **Tags:** System, Communication

Overview of all available chat commands in DMO.

### General Chat
- `/s` or `/shout` — Shout message visible to all players in the map
- `/w [name] [msg]` or `/whisper` — Private message to a specific player
- `/r [msg]` — Reply to the last whisper received
- `/p [msg]` — Party chat, visible only to party members
- `/g [msg]` — Guild chat, visible only to guild members

### Social Commands
- `/invite [name]` — Invite a player to your party
- `/trade [name]` — Send a trade request to a player
- `/block [name]` — Block a player's messages
- `/unblock [name]` — Unblock a previously blocked player
- `/friend [name]` — Add a player to your friends list

### Utility Commands
- `/where` — Shows your current map and coordinates
- `/time` — Shows the current server time
- `/sit` — Make your character sit down
- `/dance` — Make your character dance

### Guild Commands
- `/guild invite [name]` — Invite a player to your guild
- `/guild leave` — Leave your current guild
- `/guild info` — Display guild information

> **Tip:** You can click on a player's name in chat to quickly whisper them. Most commands also work with partial player names.

## 2. Currency
**Slug:** `currency` | **Tags:** System, Economy

All currency types and how to obtain them.

| Currency | How to Obtain | Primary Use |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Bit (B) | Monster drops, quests, selling items | Basic purchases, NPC shops |
| Mega (M) | 1,000 Bits = 1 Mega | Mid-tier purchases |
| Tera (T) | 1,000 Mega = 1 Tera | High-value items, trading |
| Crown (C) | Cash shop (real money) | Premium items, Cash Shop |
| DigiCore | Daily dungeons, events | Evolution items, special NPCs |
| Arena Coins | PvP Arena participation | Arena shop rewards |
| Seal Coins | Seal Master activities | Seal upgrades and materials |
| Event Tokens | Seasonal events | Event-exclusive items |

**Exchange Rate:** 1 Tera (T) = 1,000 Mega (M) = 1,000,000 Bit (B)

### Economy Tips
- The primary trading currency between players is Tera (T)
- Crown items can often be traded on the player market
- Daily dungeons are the most reliable source of DigiCore
- Event tokens typically expire when the event ends — spend them before the deadline
- Some currencies are account-bound while others are character-bound

## 3. Deck System
**Slug:** `deck-system` | **Tags:** System, Collection

Guide to the Deck system and card bonuses.

### How It Works
- Each Digimon can be scanned to obtain its card
- Cards are placed into deck slots
- Each card provides specific stat bonuses (AT, HP, DE, etc.)
- Completing card sets (e.g., all Digimon of one family) unlocks bonus effects
- Multiple deck pages can be configured for different setups

### Obtaining Cards
- Defeat a Digimon and use the Scan function
- Cards drop based on RNG — rarer Digimon have lower scan rates
- Some cards are only available from dungeon bosses or events
- Duplicate cards can be used to level up existing cards for stronger bonuses

### Card Grades
- **Normal** — Base stats
- **Rare** — 1.5× bonus
- **Epic** — 2× bonus
- **Legendary** — 3× bonus

> **Tip:** Focus on completing full family sets first, as the set bonus often outweighs individual card stats.

## 4. Digimon Arena
**Slug:** `digimon-arena` | **Tags:** System, PvP, Arena

PvP Arena rules, rankings, and rewards.

### Arena Modes
- **1v1 Arena** — Solo battles between two tamers and their Digimon
- **Tag Arena** — Team-based battles with multiple tamers per side
- **Ranked Arena** — Competitive mode with seasonal rankings and exclusive rewards

### Rules & Mechanics
- Level and stat balancing may apply in certain modes
- Cooldowns and skill usage follow PvP-specific rules
- Matches have a time limit — if time runs out, the player with more HP% wins
- Some skills have reduced effectiveness in PvP
- Burst Mode and Jogress are allowed in most modes

### Rewards
- Arena Coins — earned per match (more for wins)
- Seasonal ranking rewards (titles, exclusive items)
- Arena Shop — spend Arena Coins on evolution materials, accessories, and more
- Special titles for reaching specific ranks

> **Warning:** Arena rankings reset each season. Make sure to claim your seasonal rewards before the reset!

## 5. Digimon Attribute Arena
**Slug:** `digimon-attribute-arena` | **Tags:** System, PvP, Arena

Attribute-based Arena challenges and rewards.

### How It Works
- Only Digimon with the matching attribute can participate during each rotation
- Rotations change on a regular schedule (typically weekly)
- The attribute triangle applies: Vaccine > Virus > Data > Vaccine
- Free attribute can participate in any rotation but has no type advantage

### Rewards
- Attribute Arena Coins — unique currency for this mode
- Exclusive accessories and costumes available only through this arena
- Ranking titles specific to Attribute Arena
- Special evolution materials for certain Digimon

> **Tip:** Prepare Digimon of all three main attributes so you can participate in every rotation.

## 6. Guild
**Slug:** `guild` | **Tags:** System, Social, Guild

Guild creation, management, and guild-exclusive features.

### Creating a Guild
- Requires a specific amount of in-game currency
- Guild name must be unique and follow naming rules
- The creator becomes the Guild Master
- Initial member capacity starts small and can be expanded

### Guild Ranks
- **Guild Master** — Full control over guild settings, can promote/demote and disband
- **Vice Master** — Can invite/kick members, manage some settings
- **Officer** — Can invite members
- **Member** — Standard membership

### Guild Features
- **Guild Chat** — Private communication channel for guild members
- **Guild Buffs** — Passive stat bonuses that apply to all online members
- **Guild Raids** — Cooperative boss battles exclusive to guild members
- **Guild Level** — Guilds level up through member contributions, unlocking perks
- **Guild Storage** — Shared inventory for trading items between members

### Guild Level Benefits
- Increased maximum member capacity
- Stronger guild-wide stat buffs
- Access to higher-tier guild raids
- Additional guild storage slots
- Cosmetic guild features (emblems, badges)

## 7. Instance Dungeons
**Slug:** `instance-dungeons` | **Tags:** System, PvE, Dungeons

All instance dungeons, entry requirements, and loot.

### General Mechanics
- Each dungeon has entry requirements (level, items, daily limits)
- Dungeons are instanced — your party gets a private copy
- Most dungeons have daily or weekly entry limits
- Higher difficulty = better loot but tougher enemies
- Party size varies by dungeon (solo, 3-player, 5-player)

### Dungeon Types
- **Story Dungeons** — Follow the main storyline, lower difficulty
- **Daily Dungeons** — Repeatable content with daily entry limits, primary source of DigiCore
- **Raid Dungeons** — Group content requiring coordinated parties, best loot
- **Event Dungeons** — Time-limited dungeons available during special events
- **Challenge Dungeons** — Endgame content with extreme difficulty and exclusive rewards

### Tips
- Always check entry requirements before attempting a dungeon
- Bring appropriate consumables (HP/DS recovery items)
- Some bosses have specific mechanics — learn the patterns
- Party composition matters for harder dungeons
- Daily dungeons should be done every day for maximum DigiCore income

> **Info:** Instance Dungeon entry counts typically reset at midnight server time.

## 9. Quests
**Slug:** `quests` | **Tags:** System, PvE, Quests

Quest system overview, types, and completion tips.

### Quest Types
- **Main Quests** — Story-driven quests that progress the narrative and unlock new maps/features
- **Side Quests** — Optional quests that provide additional rewards and world-building
- **Daily Quests** — Repeatable quests that reset daily, great for steady income
- **Repeatable Quests** — Can be completed multiple times without daily limits
- **Event Quests** — Time-limited quests during special events
- **Tamer Quests** — Tamer-specific progression quests for leveling

### Quest Rewards
- EXP — For both Tamer and Digimon
- Currency — Bits, Mega, or special tokens
- Items — Equipment, consumables, materials
- Evolution Materials — Key items for Digimon evolution
- Titles — Some quests grant unique titles
- Map Access — Completing main quests unlocks new areas

### Tips
- Always pick up daily quests — they compound over time
- Main quests should be prioritized for map and feature unlocks
- Some quests have level requirements — check before attempting
- Quest NPCs are marked on the minimap with special icons
- Event quests often have the best reward-to-effort ratio

## 10. Rare Machine
**Slug:** `rare-machine` | **Tags:** System, Gacha

Rare Machine gacha system and available prizes.

### How It Works
- Insert the required token/currency into the Rare Machine
- The machine randomly selects a prize from the current prize pool
- Prize pools rotate periodically with different featured items
- Some machines have guaranteed jackpot mechanics after a certain number of tries

### Machine Types
- **Normal Rare Machine** — Uses standard tokens, common to rare prizes
- **Premium Rare Machine** — Uses premium tokens or Crown, better prize pools
- **Event Rare Machine** — Available during events with exclusive prizes
- **Mileage System** — Accumulate mileage points from pulls for guaranteed rewards

> **Warning:** The Rare Machine is a gacha system with randomized rewards. Rates for top-tier items are typically very low.

## 11. Seal Master
**Slug:** `seal-master` | **Tags:** System, Enhancement

Seal Master system, seal types, and upgrade paths.

### Seal Types
- **Leader Seal** — Provides leadership-based bonuses (AT, CT)
- **Soul Seal** — Provides defensive bonuses (HP, DE, EV)
- **Attack Seal** — Pure offensive stats (AT, AS)
- **Defense Seal** — Pure defensive stats (DE, HP, BL)

### How to Use
- Visit the Seal Master NPC in town
- Equip seals into the available seal slots on your Digimon
- Each Digimon has limited seal slots
- Seals can be removed and re-equipped freely

### Upgrading Seals
- Combine lower-grade seals to create higher-grade ones
- Upgrade success is not guaranteed — higher tiers have lower success rates
- Use protection items to prevent seal destruction on failure
- Seal materials can be obtained from dungeons, events, and the Seal Shop

> **Tip:** Focus on maxing one seal type that complements your Digimon's role before diversifying.

## 14. Digital Fusion
**Slug:** `digital-fusion` | **Tags:** System, Crafting

Digital Fusion mechanics and recipes.

### Fusion Types
- **Item Fusion** — Combine materials to create equipment, accessories, and consumables
- **Digimon Fusion (Jogress)** — Fuse two compatible Digimon into a more powerful form
- **Accessory Fusion** — Upgrade accessories by combining them with materials

### How to Fuse
1. Visit the Fusion NPC or access the fusion menu
2. Select the recipe/fusion type
3. Place required materials in the fusion slots
4. Pay the fusion fee (currency)
5. Confirm — some fusions have success/failure rates

### Success Rates
- Basic fusions have high success rates (80-100%)
- Advanced fusions may have lower rates (30-70%)
- Use success rate boosters to improve odds
- Failed fusions may consume materials without creating the result
- Some recipes guarantee success but require more materials

> **Tip:** Always check if a success rate booster is available before attempting high-value fusions.

## 16. D-Unit Hacking
**Slug:** `d-unit-hacking` | **Tags:** System, Enhancement

D-Unit Hacking process and optimization.

### How It Works
- Take your D-Unit to the Hacking NPC or use a Hacking item
- Select the D-Unit you want to hack
- Pay the required materials and currency
- The D-Unit's sub-stats are re-rolled randomly
- You can choose to keep the new stats or revert to the old ones

### Hacking Materials
- Hacking Program — Required for each hacking attempt
- Currency (Bits/Mega) — Fee per attempt
- Special Hacking Chips — For guaranteed stat improvements (rare)

### Optimization Strategy
- Focus on the stats that matter most for your Digimon's role
- AT% and CT% are generally the most valuable offensive stats
- HP% is universally useful for survivability
- Save guaranteed hacking chips for high-value D-Units
- Keep track of your best roll — don't accidentally overwrite it

> **Warning:** Always review the new stats before confirming! Once you accept the new roll, the previous stats are lost permanently.

## 17. D-Unit Fusion
**Slug:** `d-unit-fusion` | **Tags:** System, Enhancement

D-Unit Fusion mechanics and success rates.

### Fusion Process
1. Select a base D-Unit (this determines the result type)
2. Select a material D-Unit (consumed in the process)
3. Pay the fusion fee
4. The base D-Unit may level up or gain enhanced stats on success
5. On failure, the material D-Unit is consumed but the base remains unchanged

### Success Rates
| Fusion | Success Rate |
|--------|-------------|
| Tier 1 → Tier 2 | ~70% |
| Tier 2 → Tier 3 | ~50% |
| Tier 3 → Tier 4 | ~30% |
| Tier 4 → Tier 5 | ~15% |

Protection items can prevent base D-Unit degradation on failure.

> **Tip:** Use low-tier D-Units as fusion material to save costs. The material D-Unit's stats don't affect the result.

## 18. Digimon Breakthrough
**Slug:** `digimon-breakthrough` | **Tags:** System, Enhancement, Breakthrough

Breakthrough system for Digimon stat upgrades.

### How It Works
- Select a Digimon that is eligible for Breakthrough
- Gather the required Breakthrough materials
- Each Breakthrough level requires more materials than the previous one
- Successful Breakthrough permanently increases the Digimon's base stats
- Multiple Breakthrough levels are available (BT1, BT2, BT3, etc.)

### Requirements
- Digimon must be at maximum level for its current stage
- Specific Breakthrough materials (vary by Digimon)
- Currency fee increases with each level
- Some Breakthrough levels require rare materials from dungeons or events

### Stat Increases
- **BT1** — Moderate increase to HP and AT
- **BT2** — Further increases plus DE bonus
- **BT3** — Significant all-stat increase
- **BT4+** — Major stat increases, endgame territory

> **Info:** Breakthrough is one of the most impactful progression systems. Prioritize your main Digimon's Breakthrough over spreading materials across multiple Digimon.
